## 0. HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT (MODE SWITCH)
 
Pick **one** mode per training block and follow only that section. You can switch modes at any time (e.g., Double during a free week, Single during a busy work week). The weekly structure, exercises and combos are shared; only the *distribution* changes.
 
| | **MODE A — Single** | **MODE B — Double** |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions/day | 1 (evening, ~18:00) | 2 (midday + evening) |
| Midday session | — | Strength / core / mobility, 30–60 min |
| Evening session | Strength + Muay Thai combined | Muay Thai only, 60–90 min |
| Training days | 6 / week | 6 / week |
| Weekly volume | ~8–9 h | ~11–13 h |
| Best when | Busy, recovering, or tibia flaring up | Fresh, work flexible, fat-loss push |
| Jump to | [Section 2A](#2a-mode-a--single-session) | [Section 2B](#2b-mode-b--double-session) |
 
**Rule of thumb for switching:** if tibia pain exceeds 4/10, sleep is under 6 hours two nights in a row, or morning weight/mood crashes, drop to Mode A for the week.

## 2A. MODE A — SINGLE SESSION
 
One session per day at ~18:00, 6 days/week. Strength and Muay Thai are combined.
 
| Day | Focus | Session content |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Compound Strength + Power Muay Thai *(Buakaw)* | Squat 5×5, Deadlift 5×5, Floor press, Farmer's walk → heavy bag power rounds, low kicks |
| **Tue** | Conditioning GPP + Speed *(Saenchai)* | Hill sprints, tire flips, sledgehammer, burpees → speed combos, footwork |
| **Wed** | Core *(Tyson)* + Technique *(Saenchai)* | 200 sit-ups, wall sit, dead hang → pad technique, shadow boxing |
| **Thu** | Explosive Strength + Power MMA | Jump squat, clap push-up, clean & press, tire flips → power combinations |
| **Fri** | **Fight Day** | Tyson defense (slips, rolls, head movement) + Saenchai counters + 100 kicks per leg |
| **Sat** | Long Cardio + Mobility | 45–70 min easy cardio, mobility, Thai oil massage on shins |
| **Sun** | **Total rest** | Walk only. Sleep. Eat. |

## 2B. MODE B — DOUBLE SESSION
 
**Midday (~12:00–13:30, before work):** strength, core, mobility. Train 1–2 h after a light meal or fasted with coffee.
**Evening (~18:00):** Muay Thai.
 
Intensity is deliberately staggered: **one hard session per day, the other moderate or light.** Hard kicking and heavy-bag impact happen only in the evening, once per day, to protect the tibia.
 
| Day | Midday (AM) | Intensity | Evening (PM) | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Compound Strength: Squat 5×5, Deadlift 5×5, Floor press, Farmer's walk | Hard | Power Muay Thai *(Buakaw)*: heavy bag, low kicks | Moderate-Hard |
| **Tue** | Core + Mobility (30 min): sit-ups, wall sit, dead hang, hips/ankles | Light | Conditioning GPP + Speed *(Saenchai)*: hill sprints, tire flips, sledgehammer, burpees | Hard |
| **Wed** | Mobility only (20 min) or off | Very light | Technique *(Saenchai)*: pads, shadow, timing drills | Moderate |
| **Thu** | Explosive Strength: jump squat, clap push-up, clean & press, tire flips | Hard | Power MMA: combinations, no hard shin impact | Moderate |
| **Fri** | Core + Mobility (30 min) | Light | **Fight Day**: Tyson defense + Saenchai counters + 100 kicks/leg, light sparring | Hard |
| **Sat** | Long easy cardio (45–70 min) | Easy | Mobility + Thai oil massage on shins | Recovery |
| **Sun** | **Total rest** | — | — | — |
 
**Double-session rules**
1. At least **5 hours** between sessions.
2. Post-session meal within 60 minutes: protein + carbs.
3. If the evening session is "Hard", the midday one stays "Light" and vice versa (Monday and Thursday are the controlled exceptions).
4. Sleep target: 7+ hours. Double volume without sleep is just injury with extra steps.

## 3. STANCE GUIDE — SOUTHPAW (RIGHT FOOT FORWARD)
 
| Technique | Weapon | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Jab** | Right hand (lead) | Range finder, set-ups |
| **Cross** | Left hand (rear) | Power hand |
| **Lead hook** | Right hand | Short, tight |
| **Rear hook** | Left hand | Finisher |
| **Power low kick** | **Left leg** (rear) | Main weapon, hips fully rotating |
| **Teep (push kick)** | **Right leg** (lead) | Distance control, off-balancing |
 
*Orthodox sparring partners: your lead hand/foot is on their rear side. Work the angle outside their lead foot and keep your lead foot on the outside.*
 
### Core Combinations
 
| # | Combination | Sequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jab – Cross – Lead Hook – Rear Low Kick | R – L – R – L leg |
| 2 | Teep – Cross – Lead Hook – Cross | R leg – L – R – L |
| 3 | Jab – Jab – Cross – Rear Low Kick | R – R – L – L leg |
| 4 | **Knee-defense counter (Friday)** | See below |
 
**Combo 4, injury-aware counter:** when the opponent's knee rises, **withdraw the left leg → right teep → left cross → step left.** This trains the exact situation that caused the shin injury, without taking the knee on the tibia.
 
### Style Colors / Attribution
- **Tyson:** head movement, body punching, explosive power, core, conditioning
- **Buakaw:** relentless pressure, shin conditioning, heavy low kicks, clinch
- **Saenchai:** speed, feints, timing, creative counters

## 4. TIBIA / SHIN PROTOCOL
 
| Phase | Action |
|---|---|
| Pre-training | Thai oil, warm up with movement (it improves with warmth), ankle protection |
| During | Prefer straps on bags (softer surface), limit hard shin impact to **one session/day** |
| Post-training | Thai oil massage, ice only if swollen |
| Red flag | Pain >4/10 or sharp pain on impact → switch to Mode A, no hard kicks for 3–5 days |
| Pending | **Medical check** for suspected chronic periostitis |

## 5. NUTRITION TARGETS
 
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Calories (average) | **2,400–2,600 kcal/day** |
| Protein | **180–200 g/day** |
| Rest day (Sunday) | Carbs about −35%, protein unchanged |
| Pre-bed | Protein: cottage cheese, ricotta, Greek yogurt |
| Post-session | Protein + carbs within 30–60 min |
| Cheat day | One per week (Sat **or** Sun, alternating) |
 
> **Watch calories honestly:** smartwatch burn estimates tend to run high. Treat 600–800 kcal per hard session as the safer assumption.
> **Adjustment rule:** if the 7-day weight average does not fall at least 0.5 kg/week after 2 weeks, remove ~200 kcal/day (one lighter meal or skip the post-session dessert).

## 7. NON-NEGOTIABLES
 
1. Wake-up routine, every day.
2. Never skip two training days in a row. On bad days run the 30-minute minimum version.
3. One hard-impact kicking session per day, maximum.
4. Protein at every meal.
5. Sunday is rest. Rest is where the muscle and the shin repair.
*Fighters are made in the days nobody sees.*
